Operations Analytics at PropSquare

BUSINESS ANALYST · OPERATIONS & PROCESS
PropSquare LifeSpaces · Nov 2022 – Aug 2024

PropSquare manages residential communities and commercial buildings. Every month, property managers sent in 12 messy rent rolls that someone had to clean and combine by hand. I automated that, cutting monthly reporting prep from 6–8 hours to under 30 minutes, and built the Jira workflow that turned 40–50 client tickets a week into tracked field tasks.

From copy-paste to click refresh

Each building’s rent roll arrived with different column names, extra header rows, blank lines and messy text. Combining them by hand took most of a working day and invited copy-paste errors before leadership ever saw a number.

What I actually built

01

Reports that build themselves

Beyond the monthly rent rolls, I built a Power Query template for the weekly leasing pipeline report that cleaned and matched CRM exports to agent activity logs, replacing manual VLOOKUPs and cutting prep from about 4 hours to 1 hour.

02

A Jira workflow built around the field team

Client requests came in through the IWMS (integrated workplace management system). I built and owned the Jira workflow, issue types and boards for 15+ users across 3–4 managed properties: Open, Triaged/Assigned, In Progress, Pending Verification, Resolved. The Pending Verification stage was my daily check that nothing got marked done without being confirmed. I trained 15+ staff on it and wrote the team’s internal guide.

03

Using AI to sort the noise

About 150 customer-service logs came in each week: call notes, tenant feedback, walkthrough reports and vendor audits. Classifying them through the company’s ChatGPT business account cut my weekly review from 8–10 hours to 4–5.

04

Reports that moved people

I scheduled 15–20 field and operations staff daily and tracked SLAs for 5–8 facility vendors. Property managers and operations directors used my ticket-status reports to shift staff to high-traffic zones during peak hours.
