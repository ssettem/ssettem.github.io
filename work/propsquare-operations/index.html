Operations at PropSquare

BUSINESS ANALYST · JIRA ADMIN · OPERATIONS
PropSquare LifeSpaces · Nov 2022 – Aug 2024

PropSquare runs facility services for residential and commercial properties. I was one of the company’s Jira admins and the person who made sure 30–70 service tickets a day actually got done, and I automated the SLA reporting that cut breaches and response times by about 30%.

From chasing tickets to seeing them

Plumbing, electrical, maintenance and security issues arrived all day, many of them through client WhatsApp groups. Without a clear view of what was late, the only way to keep up was to chase people one by one.

How late tickets got noticed, before and after
Before: manual follow-up
Late tickets surfaced when a client complained. Nobody saw breach rates or who was sitting on too many open tickets.
→
After: about 30% fewer breaches
A Jira SLA dashboard lands with management every Monday, and a fortnightly Slack alert tags anyone with too many open tickets, with a direct Jira link.
SLA breaches and response times improved by about 30% after the automations.

What I actually did

01

Jira for every team

As one of the company’s Jira Cloud admins, I configured workflows, issue types, fields and permissions for operations, HR, payroll and finance, built from each team’s requirements. I trained users and wrote the guides and SOPs.

02

Tickets to closure

I logged issues from 70+ client WhatsApp groups as tickets, set the day’s priorities for on-ground staff, assigned outside vendors and technicians, and confirmed each job was done before closing the loop with the client.

03

Field work you can verify

With the team, we moved field work onto QR-code task checklists and an n8n WhatsApp bot that fed Jira. I monitored the real-time data, flagged missed or false “done” tasks, and standardized the end-of-day report for Facility Managers.

04

Reports, decisions and new clients

I prepared 50+ Monthly Maintenance Reports a month for client review, analyzed Facility Manager reports for leadership, and built the approved changes into Jira. I also pitched our services to builders and property owners, calling 50+ leads a week in Freshsales.
