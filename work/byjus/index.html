School Deals & Requirements at BYJU’S

BUSINESS ANALYST · PRE-SALES · REQUIREMENTS
BYJU’S · Dec 2018 – Mar 2022

BYJU’S is a K–12 education technology company. I joined as a sales associate, was promoted to senior associate, then moved into a business analyst role where I sat between the sales team and the product team: running pre-sales on 35+ school and district deals, writing the 50+ user stories developers built from, and training a sales team whose weekly revenue more than doubled in 2 months.

Fixing the pitch, carefully

One 11-rep team’s weekly revenue had stalled. I retrained their openings, presentations and objection handling, and had them test the new pitch on declined and waiting leads first, so good leads were never put at risk while the team practiced.

What I actually did as an analyst

01

Pre-sales support for 35+ school deals

For school and district deals across a metro region and surrounding districts, I built multi-grade bulk-licensing packages, low-cost plans for regional schools, ROI cases and live demos for principals and other school stakeholders, and reviewed the contracts. The reps closed the deals; together they contributed to $240K+ in school revenue.

02

Requirements developers could build from

I turned what schools needed into 50+ user stories with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria in Jira and 2–3 BRDs/FRDs per school. One example: when feedback showed students rushing through topics, I wrote the business logic for sequential unlocking, where a 90% quiz score opens the next topic.

03

The bridge between teams

I connected sales, operations, product, IT and risk stakeholders: escalating bugs, setting up dedicated support for each school and flagging payment discrepancies. I also built Tableau and Excel dashboards on calls, targets and support tickets, and segmented leads across 7 cities to focus outreach.

04

Training the sales team

I trained 40+ reps on pitching and objection handling, defined hiring criteria and interviewed sales candidates.

Where it started: sales

Before the analyst role, I spent 20 months as a Business Development Associate and then Senior Associate. I generated $200K+ in revenue through cold calls, in-home demos and family referrals, ranked among the top performers on a 20-rep team, and was promoted after 1 year. I owned the full cycle from first call through payment, including no-cost installment plans and cashback checks, and collected the post-purchase feedback that informed product improvements.
